## Before Monday

**Please do this one thing**

Evanna will divide you into your teams shortly. Once you know your group, get a quick chat going (WhatsApp or email) and start discussing the problems you'd like to tackle — between you, aim to propose one or two. There's no need to finalise anything before Monday; the goal is simply to start thinking together. The best projects start with a real frustration, not a clever idea. To find yours:

1. Start with people, not technology. Think of something slow, costly, frustrating or error-prone in your research, your field, or your community.
2. Ask: What takes far too long? What should be done but isn't? Who feels this pain, and what do they do today instead?
3. Phrase it as a question: "How might we help [who] to [do what], so that [better outcome]?"
4. Don't worry whether AI can do it — bring the problem, not the solution. Working out the "how" is exactly what we'll do together on the day.

Your problem can be one you face yourself, one you see others struggle with, or something connected to your own research — that last one isn't required, but it's often especially fertile ground.

## Two quick examples

Applying for a mortgage — slow, paperwork-heavy and confusing; people lose track of what they need and where they stand.

Hiring in the age of AI — an HR manager facing 200 applications where every CV has been AI-polished to sound perfect. How do you tell genuine fit from clever wording?

Jot down a line or two for each idea and bring them with you.
