Where Ireland's unpaid carers are most at risk.

Almost 300,000 people in Ireland provide unpaid care, often while older, poorer or more isolated themselves. We fold four need signals (CSO Census 2022 and Pobal deprivation data) into a single Vulnerability score for every one of Ireland's 18,900 small areas, readable at three levels: region, neighbourhood, and the 20 HSE areas that care providers plan around.

About these dots

Office locations, not service delivery addresses. Dots show the registered address; carers drive out to clients from there.

Many home-care offices geocode only to the town, so treat the dots as approximate.

Vulnerability × distance to the nearest carer support (home-care / Family Carers). Distance is from each area's centre to the nearest office, not where care is delivered.

20 HSE areas, ranked by market gap

Need averaged across each of the 20 HSE areas where home-support providers are licensed to operate, minus their supply. Higher score = bigger gap between need and provision. Click an area to target it.

Includes all areas; negative scores = well-supplied.
