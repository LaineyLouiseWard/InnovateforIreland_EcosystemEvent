Where care strain concentrates

See where Ireland's unpaid carers are most at risk.

Almost 300,000 people provide unpaid care — many older, poorer or isolated themselves. This map starts from the Census 2022 carer rate, then crosses it with socioeconomic risk factors to show where carers are most likely to be stretched — region by region.

What it shows

Carer rate, region by region

The share of each region's people who are unpaid carers (Census 2022) — the carer-load base measure, coloured yellow (fewest) to purple (most).

The at-risk indices

Carer load × a risk factor

Each at-risk index crosses the carer rate with one socioeconomic factor — transport isolation, lone households — to show where carers face both at once. No invented weights.

How to read it

Search, click, switch index

Pick a region to zoom in and see its figures, and switch index tabs to change which risk factor is crossed with the carer rate.
