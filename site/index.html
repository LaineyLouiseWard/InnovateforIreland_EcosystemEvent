Where care strain concentrates

See where Ireland's unpaid carers are most at risk.

Almost 300,000 people provide unpaid care — many older, poorer or isolated themselves. We combine four Census 2022 need signals into one Vulnerability score for every one of Ireland's 18,919 Census small areas — then let you drill from region, to neighbourhood, to the service areas care operators plan around.

What it shows

Vulnerability, area by area

One score from 0–100 for each small area, combining carer load, area deprivation, care demand and isolation. Higher means carers there are likelier to be stretched.

The six measures

The "why" behind the score

Vulnerability is the headline. Switch to Care load, Strain, Demand, Isolation or Service desert to see which factor is driving the need — no invented weights.
