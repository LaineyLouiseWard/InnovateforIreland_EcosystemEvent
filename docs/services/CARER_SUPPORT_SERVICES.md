# Carer support services in Ireland — collation

Reference for the carer-facing resources panel: verified contacts and links a carer could be pointed to from their area on the map. Compiled 8 June 2026.

**Verification status:** the Careline number and the 2026 financial figures were confirmed against the live source pages. The HSE `list/3/carerssupport/...` page URLs returned 404 to the automated fetcher (likely bot-blocking) and are taken from search snippets — click through to confirm before they go into the build.

## Does the HSE publish a list?

Yes, in two forms — neither is a single comprehensive national directory:

1. **National "Support Organisations" page** under *Services for Carers*:
   <https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/list/3/carerssupport/supportorganisations/>. Thin — points to only **Family Carers Ireland**, **Care Alliance Ireland**, and the **Citizens Information Board**. Sibling pages cover [health services](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/list/3/carerssupport/healthservices/), [financial supports](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/list/3/carerssupport/financial/), and [caring for older people](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/list/3/carerssupport/caringforolderpeople.html).
2. **Local/regional directory PDFs** per Local Health Office — e.g. [Longford & Westmeath](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/publications/olderpeople/hse-directory-of-supports-and-services-for-older-people-in-longford-and-westmeath.pdf), [Laois & Offaly](https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/publications/olderpeople/directory-of-health-care-and-social-care-supports-for-older-people-in-laois-and-offaly.pdf), plus a Laois/Offaly *"Who Cares" carers resource pack*. County-level, not consistent nationally.

The richest single directory is **not** the HSE's — it's [Care Alliance Ireland's List of Members](https://www.carealliance.ie/List-of-members) (~50 voluntary orgs). That asymmetry (HSE's national list is short and high-level; real depth sits in the voluntary sector and scattered county PDFs) underlines the point this map is built around: carer support is coarse and sparsely mapped.

## Frontline carer organisations (resources-panel core)

| Service | Contact | What it offers |
|---|---|---|
| **Family Carers Ireland** | National Freephone Careline **1800 24 07 24** — Mon–Thu 9:00–17:30, Fri 9:00–17:00 (Samaritans cover the same number after hours) | ~22 support centres; in-home/emergency respite, support groups, training, advocacy on entitlements, counselling, crisis support, young-carer support. Publishes the *State of Caring* survey. [familycarers.ie](https://www.familycarers.ie/carer-supports/national-freephone-careline/) |
| **Care Alliance Ireland** | [carealliance.ie](https://www.carealliance.ie/) | National network of voluntary orgs; Online Family Carer Support Group; searchable national training database; research/policy. |
| **HSELive** | **1800 700 700** (or 041 685 0300) | HSE information line for entitlements and local service access. |

## Financial supports (Dept of Social Protection — gov.ie / mywelfare.ie)

- **Carer's Allowance** — means-tested, full-time care. Income disregard rises to **€1,000 single / €2,000 couple from 2 July 2026**. [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/carers-allowance/)
- **Carer's Benefit** — PRSI-based, for those who left employment to care. Income limit rises to **€1,000/week from July 2026**. [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/carers-benefit/)
- **Carer's Support Grant** — annual lump sum each June; automatic if on Carer's Allowance/Benefit.
- **Half-rate Carer's Allowance** — payable alongside certain other welfare payments. [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/half-rate-carers-allowance/)
- **Domiciliary Care Allowance** — monthly payment for a child under 16 with severe disability. [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/disability-and-illness/domiciliary-care-allowance/)

## HSE direct services

- **Home Support Service** (home help / home care) — [hse.ie](https://www.hse.ie/eng/home-support-services/)
- **Primary Care Teams** — Public Health Nurse, OT, physio, speech & language.
- **Respite / breaks** — [citizensinformation.ie](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/health/health-services/health-services-for-people-with-disabilities/respite-care/)
- **Mental-health supports for carers, family & friends** — [www2.hse.ie](https://www2.hse.ie/mental-health/services-support/supports-for-carers-family-and-friends/)

## Information & advocacy

- **Citizens Information** — entitlements, advice, advocacy; national phone line + local centres. [Payments & supports for carers](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/payments-and-supports-for-carers/)

## Condition-specific orgs (Care Alliance members — for "nearest relevant support")

Alzheimer Society of Ireland, ALONE, Acquired Brain Injury Ireland, Enable Ireland, Irish Cancer Society, Epilepsy Ireland, Headway, and ~40 more. Full list: [carealliance.ie/List-of-members](https://www.carealliance.ie/List-of-members).
