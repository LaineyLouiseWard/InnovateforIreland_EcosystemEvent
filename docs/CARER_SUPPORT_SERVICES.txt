CARER SUPPORT SERVICES IN IRELAND — COLLATION
=============================================

Reference for the carer-facing resources panel (see _archive/CARER_WEBSITE_FEATURES.md).
Verified contacts and links a carer could be pointed to from their area on the map.
Compiled 8 June 2026.

DOES THE HSE PUBLISH A LIST?
----------------------------
Yes, in two forms — neither is a single comprehensive national directory:

1. National "Support Organisations" page under Services for Carers:
   https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/list/3/carerssupport/supportorganisations/
   Thin — points to only Family Carers Ireland, Care Alliance Ireland, and the Citizens
   Information Board. Sibling pages cover health services, financial supports, and caring
   for older people.

2. Local/regional directory PDFs per Local Health Office — e.g. Longford & Westmeath,
   Laois & Offaly, plus a Laois/Offaly "Who Cares" carers resource pack. County-level,
   not consistent nationally.

The richest single directory is NOT the HSE's — it's Care Alliance Ireland's List of
Members (~50 voluntary orgs). That asymmetry (HSE's national list is short and high-level;
real depth sits in the voluntary sector and scattered county PDFs) is the same "support is
coarse and sparse" point CARER_SCOPING.md makes.


FRONTLINE CARER ORGANISATIONS (resources-panel core)
----------------------------------------------------
Family Carers Ireland
  National Freephone Careline: 1800 24 07 24 — Mon–Thu 9:00–17:30, Fri 9:00–17:00
  (Samaritans cover the same number after hours). ~22 support centres; in-home/emergency
  respite, support groups, training, advocacy on entitlements, counselling, crisis support,
  young-carer support. Publishes the State of Caring survey.
  https://www.familycarers.ie/carer-supports/national-freephone-careline/

Care Alliance Ireland
  National network of voluntary orgs; Online Family Carer Support Group; searchable
  national training database; research/policy.
  https://www.carealliance.ie/

HSELive
  1800 700 700 (or 041 685 0300). HSE information line for entitlements and local service
  access.


FINANCIAL SUPPORTS (Dept of Social Protection — gov.ie / mywelfare.ie)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carer's Allowance — means-tested, full-time care. Income disregard rises to €1,000 single
  / €2,000 couple from 2 July 2026.
  https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/carers-allowance/
- Carer's Benefit — PRSI-based, for those who left employment to care. Income limit rises
  to €1,000/week from July 2026.
  https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/carers-benefit/
- Carer's Support Grant — annual lump sum each June; automatic if on Carer's Allowance/Benefit.
- Half-rate Carer's Allowance — payable alongside certain other welfare payments.
  https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/carers/half-rate-carers-allowance/
- Domiciliary Care Allowance — monthly payment for a child under 16 with severe disability.
  https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/social-welfare/disability-and-illness/domiciliary-care-allowance/
