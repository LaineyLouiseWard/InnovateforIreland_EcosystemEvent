THE CARER AT-RISK MAP — WEBSITE BRIEF
Innovate for Ireland · AI Build Day
8 June 2026

A short, plain-language brief to share with colleagues.


WHAT IT IS
------------------------------------------------------------
An interactive map of Ireland showing where unpaid carers are most in
need today, and where that need will grow as the population ages. Built
for carer-support services, both national and regional.


WHO IT'S FOR
------------------------------------------------------------
  - National services: the whole-country picture at a glance.
  - Regional services: zoom into one region for local, small-area detail.
Either can search for their region to jump straight there.


WHAT YOU SEE
------------------------------------------------------------
Two views you switch between:
  - NOW:  where unpaid care is most common today (Census 2022).
  - 2042: where demand is projected to grow as the population ages,
          with a slider for high / medium / low future scenarios.

Two levels you click or search through:
  - National (8 regions)
  - Regional (drill down to neighbourhood-level detail)


HOW IT WORKS (in plain terms)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - All data is free, public CSO data: Census 2022 plus the official
    population projections. No logins, no private or personal data.
  - We prepare the data in advance and bake it into the page, so the
    map loads instantly.
  - The NOW map is real, measured data. The 2042 map takes today's map
    and "ages it forward" using the CSO's official projections of how
    each region's older population grows. It is a projection under clear
    assumptions, not a crystal-ball prediction.


HOW WE'RE BUILDING IT
------------------------------------------------------------
  - A hands-on AI Build Day: we build the working website together in
    the room using Claude Code.
  - We build in layers, simplest first: the national NOW map, then the
    future view, then the scenario slider, then the regional zoom.
  - It is a prototype to show the idea, deployed as a simple public web page.


WHAT IT IS NOT (kept honest)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - It shows area patterns, not individual carers (open data cannot
    identify people).
  - The future view is a scenario, not a forecast.
  - It is a starting point built in a day, not a finished product.


THE POINT
------------------------------------------------------------
Support is usually planned from national totals that hide where strain
actually sits. This map shows where carer need concentrates now and
where it is heading, so services can plan to reach carers before they
reach crisis.
